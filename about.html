About Maker Bootcamp

Maker Bootcamp is a free, open course from University of Minnesota Libraries Creation Hubs. Each track is a line of hands-on stops — hop on anywhere, open a module's deck, and leave with something you made.

What it is

A slide-based curriculum for maker education — 3D printing, sewing, CAD, vector design, Python, digital audio, and more. Every module is a self-contained deck you can follow at your own pace, in the Creation Hubs or anywhere you have a browser.

How the tracks work

Modules are organized into three tracks. Make is hands-on tool tutorials, Design covers software and creative-technical foundations, and Create is project-building — currently under construction.

Open & remixable

Everything here is licensed CC BY 4.0 and built in the open with Quarto and RevealJS. Educators and makers are welcome to reuse, adapt, and remix the decks — see CONTRIBUTING.md if you'd like to help build the next stop.

Who makes it

Maker Bootcamp is made by University of Minnesota Libraries — Engagement, Outreach & Community Partnerships, the team behind the Creation Hubs. Stop by a hub, or start with any deck on the home page.
