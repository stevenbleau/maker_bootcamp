0.1 Inputs, Outputs, and Score

0. PINBALL PROTOTYPE

Arduino Game Loop

INPUTS
Two flipper buttons and three target switches use internal pull-up resistors.
OUTPUTS
Two servos move the flippers. Three LEDs flash when a target scores.
STATE
The score increases once per target hit and is reported in the serial monitor.
